# Meta Recovery Content Contract

This contract has highest priority while `AUTOMATION_MODE=recovery`.

## Shared trust and usefulness gate

- Every factual paragraph must name the source in natural language, for example `根據交通部 7 月 23 日公告` or `路透引述該公司財報`. Bare phrases such as `根據報導`, `媒體指出`, or `官方資料` do not count. Never invent a source. If the supplied material cannot support attribution, do not produce the post.
- Separate observed fact from interpretation. Use language such as `已知事實是…` and `這裡的判讀是…` when a reader could otherwise mistake an inference for a fact.
- Treat accusations, legal claims, public-safety claims, and political claims as attributed claims unless the supplied source proves them independently. Write `某人質疑／某機關表示`, never silently promote an allegation into `已知事實`.
- Include one concrete consequence for a named reader plus one usable next action. Avoid empty risk language, rhetorical questions, or `值得關注` conclusions.
- Avoid strategy jargon and dramatic metaphors such as `真正的賽局`, `護城河崩塌`, `底層邏輯`, or `系統性崩潰`; they reduce trust and hide the useful point.
- Do not use unsupported statistics, anonymous authority, urgency bait, outrage bait, or repetitive `神話破滅／揭露／代價` framing.
- Prefer practical current affairs, consumer trust or safety, and concrete technology consequences. Earnings and supply-chain stories need an explicit human consequence.

## Platform-native experiment

- Threads: one post per day. Lead with the useful consequence, then the source-backed fact, then one clearly labeled interpretation. Keep the tone calm and specific.
- Facebook: two evidence-backed explainers per week. Give enough context to earn trust; a source link is allowed. Do not merely lengthen the Threads copy.
- Instagram: two carousel experiments per week. The cards must carry the idea; the caption supports them. One idea per card, strong contrast, little text, and a save-worthy practical takeaway.

The experiment is testing exactly one primary hypothesis: `interest`, `trust`, `utility`, or `format`. Do not optimize all four with unrelated additions in one post.
