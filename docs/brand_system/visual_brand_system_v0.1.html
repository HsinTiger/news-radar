Visual Brand System · v0.1 · May 15 2026

A cold dispatch,
not a self‑help poster.

Brief News Radar is a contrarian business‑analysis publication. Its covers should read like a printed market dispatch — typographic, restrained, slightly hostile to decoration — and never like a LinkedIn motivational tile. This document is the visual operating system: one accent, one paper, one set of templates, five minutes per cover.

§ 4.1 / Brand DNA

Five adjectives, one posture.

Visual personality

Pick five words, not fifty. Every visual decision in this doc must defend itself against one of these. If a layout doesn't read cold, editorial, contrarian, disciplined, or analytic at a glance, it does not ship.

01 / COLD
Cold
冷感
No grins, no exclamation marks, no warmth tax. The page feels like a Tuesday‑morning briefing.
02 / EDITORIAL
Editorial
編輯感
Treated like a printed page: masthead, rule, body, byline. Type does the work, not decoration.
03 / CONTRARIAN
Contrarian
逆風
One hero claim, often inverting the consensus. The cover argues a position, never summarises.
04 / DISCIPLINED
Disciplined
紀律
Two colours, two type families, one grid. Repetition across 52 issues is the brand.
05 / ANALYTIC
Analytic
分析感
Numbers, axes, ratios are welcome — as long as they argue. Decorative charts are forbidden.

Reference map · we look like X, with a Y twist

Editorial typography(extreme‑type analyst blogs)
Take the typographic confidence — hero text dominates, imagery is optional. Reject the all‑white minimalism: we use warm paper, not screen white.
Financial press dispatch(printed newsletter feel)
Take the masthead + rule + issue stamp structure. Reject the dense column grid — we leave more negative space.
Flat‑illustration commentary(market satire publications)
Take the bold flat headline overlay. Reject the cartoon characters and the joke‑per‑cover energy.
Photo‑led essay covers(slow, atmospheric blogs)
Take the single, restrained image as anchor. Reject the moodboard‑and‑sigh tone; our captions argue.
Info‑dense market notes(retail equity newsletters)
Take the red date stamp + numeric prominence. Reject the wall‑of‑data cover — we extract one number, not twenty.

§ 4.2 / Color

One paper. One ink. One accent.

Palette & usage

Where Sienna goes

One of: (a) brand mark word "RADAR", (b) one underline beneath a hero phrase, (c) a date stamp top‑right, (d) a single numeric figure (e.g. $3.4T).

If you have used it once, you are done. Two placements = breach.

Where Sienna never goes

Body copy, deck text, kickers across >3 words, drop shadows, gradients, photo tinting, page borders, decorative shapes, icon fills.

If unsure: leave it black.

When Alert Red replaces Sienna

Triggers: crash, sell‑off, bankruptcy, layoffs, war/sanction, regulatory action. Replaces Sienna for that one cover — never coexists with it.

Budget: < 10% of issues per quarter or it loses signal.

Light vs Dark covers

Default is paper. Invert (Ink background, Paper text) for obituaries, year‑end retrospectives, and "the case against X" pieces. Not more than 1 in 8.

Dark covers keep the same accent rules. No additional colours unlocked.

§ 4.4 / Layout grid

1456 × 816, four fixed zones.

Canvas spec

The grid below is identical on every template. Hero text always lives in Zone C. The masthead never moves. New designers should not invent new zones — they should choose which zones to leave empty.

Zone A · Masthead1328 × 44 px · top@64
Zone B · Imagery / Diagram (optional)1328 × 392 px
May be empty (Template 1), partial (right half), or full‑bleed bg
Zone C · Hero text880 × 310 px max
200–280 px · 1–3 lines · always left‑aligned · always bottom‑seated
Zone D · Footer1328 × 40 px · bot@64
Canvas

1456 × 816 px · 16:9 · Substack cover spec · export PNG ≤ 4 MB / JPG q92

Safe zone

64 px margin on all sides. Nothing semantic outside.

Column grid

12 cols · 16 px gutter · 100 px col‑width inside safe zone. Hero spans 8 cols.

Baseline

8 px grid. All type sits to baseline. Hero leading is .92, snap to grid only at meta sizes.

§ 4.5 / Discipline

Five to keep. Five to refuse.

Do / Don't

If a cover violates any "don't", it ships only after explicit override from editor. The "do" column is the cover's pass/fail rubric — five out of five.

Always do.
5 / 5
  1. Hero text dominates. Largest object on the page, every time. If imagery is louder than type, cut imagery.Test: squint. The headline still wins.
  2. One subject, one claim. Cover argues a single position. No "and also".If you need a comma in the hero, you have two covers.
  3. Two colours, period. Paper + Ink, plus one Sienna placement. Reserved Red replaces Sienna, never joins it.Multi‑colour reads as "tile", not "dispatch".
  4. Masthead + issue stamp visible. NEWS RADAR top‑left, ISSUE № top‑right, every cover.52 issues in a feed must read as one publication.
  5. Read at 60 × 40 px. Open the thumbnail row. If you can't read the hero, the cover is rejected.No exceptions, no "but the layout is nice".
Never do.
5 / 5
  1. No cartoon people. No mascots, no LinkedIn talking heads, no illustrated 主角.Wrong publication, wrong reader.
  2. No gradients, glows, or 3D. Flat fills only. No drop shadows on type. No gradient overlays on photos.All of these read as 2017 startup deck.
  3. No emoji, no exclamation marks. No 💡 🚀 🔥, no "！", no quoted hot‑take headlines.We are not a 雞湯 channel.
  4. No decorative borders or frames. No double rules, no ornament, no rounded card with shadow.The masthead rule is the only frame the cover gets.
  5. No AI‑generated imagery. Especially no AI‑rendered Chinese characters. Stock photo, icon, or pure type only.Brittle quality + brand risk + slow workflow.

§ 4.7 / Workflow

Title in. Cover out. Five minutes.

Handoff · for PM agent

A decision tree the PM agent runs in order. Each step has one question, one answer bucket, and one output. If the agent ever stops to think creatively, it has failed — the system did.

Step 01
Is this a market crash / sell‑off / bankruptcy / sanction piece?
YES → swap accent → Alert Red. NO → keep Sienna.
0:30
Step 02
Does the piece centre on a chart with ≤ 2 series and a source?
YES → Template 03 Chart.
→ T03 / CHART
1:00
Step 03

YES → Template 05 Diagram.
→ T05 / DIAGRAM

Hero‑text extraction · ≤ 8 字

  1. Find the verb of disagreement (不是, 救不了, 等不到, 永遠錯).
  2. Find the noun of consensus being attacked (AI, 降息, 複利, 市場).
  3. Compress to a complete short sentence, ending with 句號 or comma cliff.
  4. If > 8 字, split across two lines at the verb.
  5. One character or one short phrase carries the Sienna accent.
「為什麼便宜的東西反而最貴」→越便宜，越貴。
「Nvidia 不是泡沫、是基礎建設」→AI 不是泡沫。
「複利是世界第八奇蹟、但你不會等」→你等不到複利。
「降息不會救你的存款」→降息救不了你。
「Costco 的反消費主義悖論」→Costco 悖論。
「市場永遠對、直到它錯」→市場永遠對，直到它錯。
