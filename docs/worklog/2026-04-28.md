## Spec-interpretation flag (PM ratification needed before Item 7)

Spec §3 Item 6 step 7 literal formula
``mean × (count_at_or_above_T / total_count)`` is algebraically
``sum_in_tail / N``, monotonically non-increasing in T for non-negative
weights — does NOT balance per-post quality with publish volume as
labeled. Item 6 implements the **mean-of-tail** interpretation
(matches the spec's named metric `engagement_per_published_post`)
plus a `MIN_TAIL_FOR_FIT=5` sparse-tail guard. Documented in the
audit + System_Architecture §6.5; flagged here for PM sign-off.
